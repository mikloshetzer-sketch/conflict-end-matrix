Analyst Interpretation

What are we seeing?

The summary shows the balance of non-kinetic events in the selected period.

How is it calculated?

The summary cards use the latest 30-day window. Diplomatic events, escalation-oriented statements and ceasefire/de-escalation signals come from the Conflict End Matrix event layer. Military events come from the cleaned kinetic_events.json dataset generated from ME Security Monitor / IranStrike.

Conclusion

The balance helps show whether the political and information environment is dominated by diplomacy, escalation-oriented signalling or de-escalation efforts.

Events are grouped by UTC calendar day. Diplomatic rows summarize Conflict End Matrix non-kinetic events; Military rows summarize cleaned kinetic events.
