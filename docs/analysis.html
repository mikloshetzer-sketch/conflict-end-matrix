What are we seeing?

The summary shows the balance of non-kinetic events in the selected period.

How is it calculated?

Events are counted from the currently filtered timeline. Diplomatic events, escalation-oriented statements and ceasefire/de-escalation signals are separated by event type. The military-event counter is reserved for the later kinetic data layer and is currently zero unless linked military data are added.

Conclusion

The balance helps show whether the political and information environment is dominated by diplomacy, escalation-oriented signalling or de-escalation efforts.

How is it calculated?

Events are filtered by timestamp, actor and event type. Where a political or diplomatic signal is linked to a later military event, elapsed time is calculated from the two timestamps. A confidence label describes the strength of the identified relationship; it does not prove causation.

Conclusion
